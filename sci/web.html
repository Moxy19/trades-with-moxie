Live signals, graded and explained

Each setup follows the Synthetic Indices Trader method: the 1D/4H structure sets the direction (low then higher low = buy wave), the opposing move is mapped, and a signal fires when price breaks back through a past floor or ceiling inside it and retests it on the 5m. The SL sits beyond the previous floor and TP1 is the next floor in the way. Every trade is tracked live from entry to its final TP or SL, with the same 3-position management as Royal Trades, so the two can be compared fairly.

Signals are educational, not financial advice. Risk 1–2% per trade at most. You make every trading decision.

The robot is educational software, not financial advice. Automated trading can lose money fast. Results in backtests and Paper mode leave out spreads, commission and slippage. Run it on a demo account first, keep risk at 1–2% per trade, and only trade money you can afford to lose.
